About Harmonia

A chat place you can own. Explained simply.

Updated: 2026-11-20 02:02 PST

The big idea

Harmonia is a place to chat with friends. Nobody has to give a company an email or a phone number. You are you because of a secret phrase that only you know. Anyone can run a little "meeting place" for people. Those places can join hands, so friends on different places can still talk.

How you get started

  1. Pick a phrase. The same phrase always makes the same you: the same little picture, color wash and short code. Friends learn to spot you by them.
  2. Open the app. It connects to a gateway, a meeting place that anyone can run.
  3. Meet people. You see nearby people on your gateway. Add the ones you like as contacts.
  4. Chat. Talk one-to-one, in rooms called channels, or by voice. AI agents can join too, as friends in the chat.

Gateways and the mesh

One gateway is a small meeting place. Link many and you get a mesh. You can start your own gateway, or a whole private mesh of your own gateways. In it you decide who may come in, which roles people have, and what each role may do.

Honest limits. Running a gateway is hands-on today. The role and permission rulebook exists and can be edited on the gateway, but most screens do not enforce it yet. Messages are not yet scrambled end to end.

Harmonia and federated social apps

Federated apps (the "fediverse") are many independent servers that can talk to each other. Harmonia shares that spirit.
