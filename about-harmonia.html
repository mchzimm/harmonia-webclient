About Harmonia

A chat place you can own. Explained simply.

Updated: 2026-11-21 18:55 PST

The big idea

Harmonia is a place to chat with people: friends, family, co-workers, teams and more. Nobody has to give a company an email or a phone number. You are you because of a secret phrase that only you know. Anyone can run a little "meeting place" for people. Those places can join hands, so people on different places can still talk.

How you get started

  1. Pick a phrase. The same phrase always makes the same you.
  2. Your look is made. It has two parts: a blockie, a little pixel-art picture that is your avatar, and a 6-digit code (hex). Together they point to you alone, one by sight and one by text. People learn to spot you by them.
  3. Open the app. It connects to a gateway, a meeting place that anyone can run.
  4. Meet people. You see nearby people on your gateway. Add the ones you like as contacts.
  5. Chat. Talk one-to-one, in rooms called channels, or by voice. AI agents can join too, as chat bots.

Gateways and the mesh

One gateway is a small meeting place. Link many and you get a mesh. You can start your own gateway, or a whole private mesh of your own gateways. In it you decide who may come in, which roles people have, and what each role may do.

A private gateway keeps a whitelist: a list of the people allowed in. A new private gateway starts with an empty list, so nobody gets in. The gateway owner adds a person by their 6-digit code on the gateway control screen. Only then can that person's app connect there.

Honest limits. Running a gateway is hands-on today. The role and permission rulebook exists and can be edited on the gateway, but only a few places check it so far, such as who may add an AI agent. Messages are already encrypted while they travel, between your app and a gateway and between gateways. True end-to-end encryption, where only the sender and the receiver can read a message and not even a gateway can, is not finished yet.

Harmonia and federated social apps

Federated apps (the "fediverse") are many independent servers that can talk to each other. Harmonia shares that spirit.
