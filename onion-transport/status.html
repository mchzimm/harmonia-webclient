Note: a page served over https:// (GitHub Pages) can only open wss:// (TLS) WebSockets — browsers block plain ws:// from an https origin (mixed content). The bridge (harmonia-onion-bridge) currently listens on plain ws://, so this check will only succeed when: the page itself is opened over http:// (e.g. loaded from a LAN address), or the bridge is fronted with TLS. Same-origin http:// pages have no such restriction.
